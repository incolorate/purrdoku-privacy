Pawfect Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Pawfect Sudoku is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partners handle, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email negrupaul95@yahoo.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, best times, cats, treats, accessories, streak) and your settings are saved only on your device. We don't run servers, you don't need an account, and we don't receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Pawfect Sudoku is free and shows rewarded ads only: an ad plays only when you tap to watch one, for extra treats, a hint or to keep your streak. Ads are provided by Unity's LevelPlay ad mediation (ironSource Ads) and Unity Ads. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about your device and the app (model, operating system, language, diagnostics);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, how long an ad played) and app usage times.

They use it to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and keep their services secure. This data is sent encrypted. How Unity handles it is described in the Unity Privacy Policy, which also explains how to ask Unity to access or delete your data.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads. The first time you open Pawfect Sudoku it asks whether ad partners may use your data to personalize ads. If you say no, ads are not personalized, and we tell our partners that you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can change this any time in the game's Settings (“Personalized ads”).
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Not watching ads. You never have to watch an ad to play.

Delete your data

How to delete the data connected to Pawfect Sudoku:

  • Game data on your phone (progress, cats, treats, settings): uninstall Pawfect Sudoku, or clear its storage in Settings › Apps › Pawfect Sudoku › Storage › Clear data. It is deleted at once; nothing is kept by us.
  • Data held by our ad partners (advertising ID, approximate location, device and ad-interaction data): email negrupaul95@yahoo.com with the subject “Pawfect Sudoku data deletion” and the advertising ID shown in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads). We will pass the request to Unity (LevelPlay / Unity Ads) within 30 days. You can also ask Unity directly, as described in the Unity Privacy Policy. Unity may keep some data for as long as the law requires (for example for fraud prevention); its policy explains how long.
  • Start fresh any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

Pawfect Sudoku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed from our ad partners, contact us and we'll help you reach them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Material changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.
